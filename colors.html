Rules summary

  1. Six rotators form four scoring triangles: ABC, BDE, BCE and CEF.
  2. Click one rotator: chosen wheel advances +1; all connected neighbors go −1.
  3. After the move, every newly created monochromatic triangle gives one token of that color.
  4. Repeated colors are allowed, but victory requires tokens of 3 different colors.
  5. Each player may once per game replace one wheel with a different color cycle.
